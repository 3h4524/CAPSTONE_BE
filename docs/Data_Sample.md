## Aggregated Research Insights

- Personalized products have the strongest recurring signal across memorial, wedding, family, baby, teacher, pet, and housewarming niches.
- Outdoor products frequently use mountain, camping, hiking, national park, adventure, and road trip themes.
- Vintage, retro, rustic, minimalist, and cottagecore are recurring visual directions.
- Products tied to a clear event or deadline have strong seasonal demand: Christmas, Valentine's Day, Halloween, graduation, weddings, back-to-school, and baby showers.
- Low-cost digital downloads and small personalized gifts can work as impulse purchases or add-on products.
- Sentimental products often show high favorite and view counts because buyers are purchasing for emotional milestones.
- Recommended product strategy: combine one clear audience identity, one recognizable visual style, one specific occasion, and optional personalization.
- All recommendations are hypotheses based on the sample dataset and should be validated with fresh marketplace data before production decisions.
